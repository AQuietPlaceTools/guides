### **If Riven is not there:**

Your job is to clear adds until Riven shows up.

Once Riven has arrived:

- damage the boss by shooting the Taken orb in her mouth. The damage window is about 20 seconds. At the end of this window, the team wipes
- One (or 2 if you're more comfortable) of the team members must take care of destroying the eyes called out by the other team. A sniper rifle is recommended.
- Destroying the eyes ends the damage phase and prevents the team from wiping.
- If an eye other than the ones called out is destroyed, the team wipes.

Once all of this is done, activate and take the elevators to go up to the next floor

### **How do you activate the elevators?**

During the add clear, an Eye of Riven spawned.

- One player stands on the platform opposite to where Riven appears, and another one picks up the Eye.
- The person with the Eye looks at the person on the platform and calls out the symbol that appeared under them
