Here is how the encounter goes:

- 1L grabs the first orb to start the encounter
- 2L and 3L grab one orb each on the left
- meanwhile, 1R grabs 2 orbs on the right and gets caged by the boss
- To free them, 2R picks up an "Eye of Riven", an item dropped by Taken Captains, then uses their grenade button on the caged person to free them.

**Note:** When the person is freed, the Taken Strength is transferred. That means the freed person goes down to Taken Strength 0 (they lose the debuff) and the uncager goes up to Taken Strength x2.

- once freed, 1R grabs 2 orbs again
- 1R or 2R gets caged
- 3R picks up an Eye of Riven and frees the caged person (for this example, let's assume 1R got caged a 2nd time)
- 1R, the player who was just freed, grabs 2 orbs again to get to Taken Strength x2
- meanwhile, 2L and 3L grab one orb each to get to Taken Strength x2
- When the middle orb respawns, 1L grabs it to start the damage phase.

Once the damage phase has started, get behind Morgeth, next to the door, and light him up.

GG, third encounter done.
