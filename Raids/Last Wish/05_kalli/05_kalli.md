## **__1st encounter: Kalli, the Corrupted__**

Let's get down to business with the 1st encounter.

Kalli is the first boss of this raid.

In the middle of the room is a platform, the boss stands in the center of this platform, and all around it there are a bunch of plates, 9 in total.

On this platform, there are 3 pillars displaying 3 symbols (2 x 3 symbols, 1 symbol per pillar) (see picture)

The goal is to find the 6 plates matching the middle symbols and put a fireteam member on each one.

When the encounter starts, every member steps on their plate. The plate is split into 3 zones, 2 Taken zones and one normal zone: the player must absolutely stay on the normal zone of their plate, otherwise they die. But this normal zone will move, so keep your eyes on the plate and always position yourself on the normal zone. Once your plate is captured, a Taken Knight will spawn. **you must kill the Knight because it has an impact on the damage phase**

**Note:** while you are on your plate, the boss may teleport onto you. If that happens, don't panic, the boss isn't very dangerous. Damage her with your primary and secondary weapons, and she will eventually leave.
