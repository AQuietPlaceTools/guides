## **__5th encounter: Riven of a Thousand Voices__**

Yay, here we are at the final boss! Riven of a Thousand Voices, the last Ahamkara, and killing her is our calling.

There are 2 ways to do Riven: the Cheese version and the Legit version.

### **__Riven Cheese__**

In the room there are 6 plates, stepping on these 6 plates starts the encounter.

Once the encounter has started, head to the door located below the left plates. **Warning:** The door eventually closes, so don't waste time getting there.

Go through the door and head to the room at the end of the corridor.

In this room there will be a few adds, but above all a big black sphere. Watch this black sphere closely for a few seconds (keep watching it until 5 seconds after the adds spawn), you may see Riven's shadow pass through it. If Riven's shadow passes, you can stay in this room.

If you don't see Riven's shadow, turn around and hug the back wall of the previous room. You should get the message "Joining allies", and eventually get teleported to the spawn. Once up there, head to the opening on the right and go all the way down.

**Damage**: When cheesing Riven, swords are usually the go-to. Take The Lament with Tractor Cannon, place a Well of Radiance near Riven's right forearm and go to town. A Stasis Titan with Tractor Cannon and Synthoceps standing near Riven's fingers can also deal a staggering amount of damage.

When Riven's first health bar is depleted, the fireteam gets teleported to the Ascendant Plane. Completing the jumping section sends the fireteam back in front of Riven, where you can finish her off.

When her health hits 0, go into her mouth and head to her heart to finish her once and for all.
