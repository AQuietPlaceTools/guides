Just like in the third encounter, each player can grab a maximum of 2 orbs, the third one being fatal.

Keep in mind that only the last orb picked up resets the carrier's timer.

### **For example:**

If there are 4 orbs in the room, 3 orbs can be picked up instantly. The 4th must be taken at the last moment (ideally when the carrier's timer is below 3 seconds)

By convention, the first person teleported is always the one who grabs the last orb.

Repeat all of these steps until the end of the encounter.
