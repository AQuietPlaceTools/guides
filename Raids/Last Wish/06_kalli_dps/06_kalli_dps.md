### **__The damage phase__**

Once your plate is captured, head to the middle for the damage phase.

It is a classic damage phase: deal as much damage as possible to the boss within a limited time.

After a few seconds, the boss will raise her arms and prepare her wipe mechanic. At that moment, doors will open beneath the boss so you can take cover. One door opens per plate whose Knight was killed, so you can have up to 6 doors.

(see picture)

Each player gets into a door to survive. If a door is taken by 2 players, it won't close and both players will die. Any player outside a door when the wipe happens will die as well.

When the doors open again, the damage phase continues, followed by another door phase.

This damage/door sequence happens 3 times in total.

If Kalli is still breathing after these 3 phases, start everything over from the beginning.

Otherwise, congratulations, you have defeated Kalli!
