## **__2nd encounter: Shuro Chi, the Corrupted__**

Shuro Chi is the second encounter of the raid, and the 2nd boss.

It's a mix of add clear, damage and puzzle. When the encounter starts, wreak havoc on the adds, not a single one must survive, then get close to Shuro Chi.

Around Shuro Chi are 3 plates, on which relics, aka peanuts, will spawn.

3 players stand next to the plates, pick up the peanut and step on their respective plate at the same time. **__Warning:__** __you take damage while standing on the plate, so don't rush__ onto it or you risk **__Dying__**

With the peanut, each player must shoot the person on the plate to their right, forming a triangle, until the boss's shield drops. Once the shield is down: damage. The goal is to take one health segment off the boss before she wipes the fireteam.

Repeat this whole process 2 times per floor.

Once the 2nd damage phase of the floor is done, you will enter a room with 9 plates and 3 lit lanterns.

First, look at the left lantern. You will see a symbol on this lantern, with 4 greyed-out parts. The goal is to stand on the plates matching the greyed-out parts, as in the example below:
