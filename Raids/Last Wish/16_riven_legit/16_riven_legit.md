### **__Riven Legit__**

First of all, form 2 teams of 3 people and step on the plates to start the encounter.

Each team will go into a room.

The goal of the encounter is to play ping pong with Riven.

**Warning:** in this encounter, avoid Unraveling Rounds and Jolt effects, you could get your team killed (see below)

Once each team is in its room, there are 2 cases:

- Riven is there
- Riven is not there

### **If Riven is there:**

Once again there are 2 cases, but both lead to the same point.

- The flamethrower: Riven will breathe fire at you from her mouth, dealing enough damage to her mouth while it glows will stun the boss
- The tentacle: Stand under the tentacle to draw aggro so Riven slams the ground. Don't stay under the tentacle when it slams. Dealing enough damage to the tentacle while it glows and is on the ground will stun the boss.

When the boss is stunned:

- Look closely at Riven's eyes, 2 of them will have a flame effect around them.
- Call out the 2 eyes you see
