## **__6th encounter: Queen's Walk__**

You thought it was over? hahaha too bad for you, it's not. But don't worry, you're close to the finish line.

This last encounter is a relay.

A random person is chosen to be the chosen one, meaning this person will carry the Heart of Riven.

Once the heart is picked up, the encounter starts, and an aura appears around the heart. Being inside the aura prevents and cleanses a debuff. This debuff stacks and increases over time while you are outside the aura. This debuff darkens your screen and blocks your vision. If your screen goes completely black, you die.

The person carrying the heart has a timer. It is essential that this countdown is called out loud, because once it hits 0, the carrier gets teleported to another room. If you are too close to the carrier, you will be teleported along with them. Avoid this as much as possible, because with too many teleports, the encounter can't be completed

**Warning:** every dead player will also be teleported to the other room.

When the carrier gets teleported, a new carrier is chosen by the heart; this new carrier picks up the relic and continues on the path. Once again, it is **crucial** that the countdown is called out loud.

### **The other room:**

The players in the other room are there to let the carrier move with the heart for longer. During the walk, Taken orbs will appear in this room; going through an orb resets the carrier's timer.

**Warning:** each timer can only be reset once

The more people get teleported, the more orbs there will be to grab in the room.
