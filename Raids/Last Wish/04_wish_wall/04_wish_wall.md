## **__Raid specificity__**

Before going through the different encounters of the raid, it is worth mentioning the biggest specificity of this raid, something unique in the game.

__*Last Wish*__ has a unique mechanic called the Wall of Wishes.

### **__What is the Wall of Wishes for?__**

The wall lets you enter what could be compared to cheat codes.

Enter the code, step on the middle plate and the code is validated.

When the code is validated, a message appears. If the code is wrong, the person who stepped on the plate dies.

You can find the different codes on this website:

[Here are the Wall of Wishes codes](<https://www.next-stage.fr/2018/09/destiny-2-comment-activer-les-15-voeux-du-mur-des-souhaits.html>)

Not all codes are worth it. However, a few of them are worth a look:

- Wish 1 grants an Ethereal Key that opens an additional chest at the end of the raid. **__WARNING__** this wish is only available once per account
- Wish 2 spawns an additional chest after the 3rd encounter. This chest requires a Glittering Key to open, a key that is only obtained at the end of the raid
- Wish 3 grants an emblem
- Wish 4 takes you straight to the 2nd encounter, Shuro Chi
- Wish 5 takes you straight to the 3rd encounter, Morgeth
- Wish 6 takes you straight to the 4th encounter, the Vault
- Wish 7 takes you straight to the 5th encounter, Riven
- Wishes 8 to 12 are troll wishes, they are useless
- Wish 13 triggers a "Petra's Run", i.e. a flawless run. 1 death = the whole fireteam goes back to orbit
- And finally, wish 14 spawns eggs that must be destroyed with Wish-Ender (exotic Kinetic bow)
