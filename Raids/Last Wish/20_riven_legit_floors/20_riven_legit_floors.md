The whole fireteam is reunited.

Riven will appear in 3 spots in the room: damage Riven's mouth and note the eyes at each appearance (2 per appearance)

On the 4th appearance: damage Riven and destroy all 6 eyes.

If you haven't beaten Riven, step on the plates to go back down, but this time, during the descent, destroy the Taken blights.

Start everything over from the beginning until Riven is beaten.
