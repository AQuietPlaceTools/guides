## **__4th encounter: The Vault__**

This is the most complex encounter of the raid: the Vault.

The encounter is made of 3 rooms: Spawn, Rock (because there's a big rock) and Forest (there's a tree).

We'll assign 2 people to each room: a runner and an add clearer.

### **Runner**:

- The 3 runners step on the middle plates to start the encounter and look closely at the machine in front of them.
- 3 symbols will appear
- The Spawn player (S from now on) calls out which symbol is in the middle of their row.
- The Rock and Forest players (R and F) look at their left and right symbols to figure out who has the symbol called by S. Let's assume R has the symbol.
- R calls out which side the symbol is on. Here it will be left, for the example.
- This means R's plate becomes Penumbral. If the symbol had been on the right, R's plate would have been Antumbral.
- R calls out which symbol is in the middle of their row. For the example, S will have the symbol.
- S calls out which side the symbol is on. For the example, it will be on the right.
- S's plate therefore becomes Antumbral.
