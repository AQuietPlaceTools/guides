4 players stand near the plates and step on them when everyone is ready. If all the right plates are taken, the lantern will be completely filled, and the middle lantern will light up (it's always in the same order: left -> middle -> right). Repeat this section for all 3 lanterns.

**__Warning__**: a player cannot step on the same plate twice during a floor. If a player has to step on a plate they have already stepped on, they must call it out and swap with another plate runner, for example: swap 1/2 (plate runner 1 goes on plate 2 and plate runner 2 goes on plate 1).

Once all 3 lanterns are done, go up to the next floor using the plates that will come down, and repeat everything from the beginning until Shuro Chi is defeated.

Well done, you have defeated the second boss!
