## **__3rd encounter: Morgeth, the Spirekeeper__**

3rd boss: Morgeth aka the big pile of crap.

Split the fireteam in 2: 3 people on the left and 3 people on the right.

### **Left side:**

- one person will be the "trigger", meaning they will grab the first Taken orb in the middle to start the encounter, and the last one to start the damage phase
- the other 2 will clear adds and, at first, grab **__only 1 orb each__**

**Note:** you need to jump into the orbs to pick them up.

### **Right side:**

- only 1 person will be in charge of grabbing **__all__** the orbs on the right.
- The other 2 will be add clearer and uncager.

Picking up an orb gives the "Taken Strength" debuff. This debuff stacks up to 2 times maximum, meaning each person can take 2 orbs maximum: if Taken Strength reaches x3 -> instant death.

I'll shorten the names to 1R 2R 3R for the right-side players (same principle for the left-side ones: 1L 2L 3L)
